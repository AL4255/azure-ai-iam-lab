Quarterly Operations Report - Q3

Summary
Platform uptime was 99.94% for the quarter, up from 99.81% in Q2. The two
incidents both came from expired TLS certificates; certificate renewal is now
automated and monitored.

Highlights
- Migrated the reporting service to managed identities; no more stored keys.
- Cut cloud spend 12% by auto-shutting down dev VMs overnight.
- Closed 41 of 47 open security findings. The remaining 6 are low severity.

Next quarter
- Roll out Conditional Access for all admin accounts.
- Move build logs to central Log Analytics.
- Review RBAC assignments and remove unused ones.
